THARAKA SUB-COUNTY: PRELIMINARY LAND-COVER TREND SCREENING
===========================================================
Source: MODIS MCD12Q1 Version 6.1, IGBP LC_Type1, nominal 500 m pixels.
Requested-year mapping: requested 2000 uses source year 2001; requested 2025 uses source year 2024.
These are product-derived area estimates, not locally accuracy-assessed Landsat classifications.

Decision-screening threshold: absolute class-share change >= 2 percentage points.
This threshold is a configurable follow-up flag, not a statistical test.

BASELINE-TO-ENDPOINT CLASS CHANGES (source years 2001 to 2024):
No class crossed the configured share-change screening threshold.

MAPPED-AREA COVERAGE CHECK (screening threshold: 95% of boundary area):
No source year fell below the 95% mapped-area screening threshold, or coverage could not be computed.

RECOMMENDED USE:
1. Use the output as a broad screening view of class composition and candidate changes.
2. Inspect flagged classes on maps and confirm them using higher-resolution imagery and local knowledge.
3. Treat settlement and small fragmented patches especially cautiously: a 500 m product is too coarse for parcel- or village-scale decisions.
4. Before formal planning or high-confidence claims, build an independent reference sample, produce a locally appropriate Landsat classification, and report accuracy and area uncertainty.
5. Do not infer causes of a change from this descriptive time series alone.
