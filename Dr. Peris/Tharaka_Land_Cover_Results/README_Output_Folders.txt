Tharaka 30 m Landsat analysis outputs
figures/16_Seasonal_Scene_Maps: 16 individual false-colour maps (PNG and PDF), contact sheets and trend plots.
figures/Tharaka_30m_Named_LandCover_Area_Trends.png/.pdf: primary outcome figure showing mapped area (ha) by provisional land-cover category and target year.
figures/Tharaka_30m_Named_LandCover_Share_Trends.png/.pdf: primary outcome figure showing each category's share of classified area by target year.
figures/Tharaka_30m_LandCover_Maps_All_Years.png/.pdf and 30m_LandCover_Maps/: four-year category map panel and native 30 m category GeoTIFFs.
tables/Tharaka_30m_LandCover_Trend_Summary.csv: primary trend outcome with area, mapped-area share, study-area share, net change since 2000 and change between consecutive target years.
30m_Cluster_Maps/: native 30 m cluster GeoTIFFs and display maps.
tables/: selected scene inventory, cluster areas, index summaries, cluster feature profiles, coverage diagnostics, change tables and crosswalk.
diagnostics/: pixel-coverage, grid, processing and run diagnostics.
exports/: final boundary and convenient map/contact-sheet exports.
01_data/landsat/stac_scene_cache/: cached, cropped 30 m per-scene reflectance data.
01_data/landsat/seasonal_composites_30m/: final QGIS-clipped six-band seasonal composites.
No Earth Engine or Google Drive access is required by this document.
